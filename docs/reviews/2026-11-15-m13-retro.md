## 7. Guard proposals, ranked by what they would have saved

1. **Constraint, geometry not tokens** (I1, M1, D3, D2). Put in the Global Constraints of the next plan, and in `CONTRIBUTING.md` under E2E: "A focus, overlap or containment requirement is tested by measured boxes (outline outer box inside the surface and viewport; adjacent boxes do not intersect; elements share a row), over every focusable or sibling element, never by a computed colour or token. A locator helper that matches zero elements fails the test." Would have saved the final review's top finding, the batched-fix and delta r2 rounds (about 187k tokens, 9% of a window) and the 3h50m pause. Cost is one sentence.
2. **Test, every variant at the breakpoints** (I2, M2). In `web/e2e/stage.spec.ts`, loop the legend-within-corner and ranges-not-overlapping check over one measure per unit (per-100k, percent, dollars, count) at 900x800, 1100x800 and 320x640. #331 added part of this; assert the 320 and per-unit cases. The wrapping text is data-driven, so one measure proves nothing.
3. **Checklist line, fix rounds** (D1). In `workflow/prompts/batched-fix.md`: "After each CSS fix, re-measure the touched region at 320, 375 and 1440 and compare the header row count and the stage top with the pre-fix values; do not rely on the fix's own assertion." This would have removed one fix round and one delta review (about 187k tokens, 9% of a window).
4. **Checklist line, vocabulary greps** (T7 round 2). In `workflow/prompts/task-review.md` and the plan template: "A retired-term sweep greps case-insensitively over all of `web/src` excluding tests, and the reviewer re-runs it." Saves about 171k tokens and 4% of a window.
5. **Test, closes lines** (milestone-pr). In `workflow/tests/test_milestone_pr.py`: assert that the generated `Closes` lines include every issue in the plan's issue map, backlog issues included. Prevents the manual repair the final review asked for.
6. **Checklist line, spec enumerations** (M5). In `workflow/prompts/task-review.md`: "For each spec sentence that lists variants, states or breakpoints, confirm each is built or the narrowing is recorded in the spec's as-built section." Small saving, only a triage item.
7. **Test, locator non-vacuity** (D2). Covered by guard 1's last sentence, so no separate guard.

No guard is proposed for M3, M4, M6 or M7: their follow-up issues already exist or the finding costs less than the guard would (anti-goal 4).
